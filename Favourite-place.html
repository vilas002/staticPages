Favourite places

Taj Mahal

• The Taj Mahal is an internationally renowned white marble monument built along the banks of the Yamuna River.It was commissioned in 1632 by the Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan to house the tomb of his favourite wife, Mumtaz Mahal.

Golden Temple

• The Harmandir Sahib, popularly known as the Golden Temple, is the holiest pilgrimage site in Sikhism.Its stunning upper floors are overlaid with pure gold leaf, creating a beautiful reflection in the surrounding holy pool (Amrit Sarovar).

Varanasi

• Varanasi is one of the world's oldest continuously inhabited cities and is considered the spiritual heart of India.Situated along the banks of the sacred River Ganges, it is a major hub for Hindu pilgrimage, rituals, and meditation.

India Gate

The India Gate is a majestic 42-metre-high triumphal arch located in the heart of New Delhi.it stands as a solemn war memorial dedicated to the thousands of Indian soldiers who lost their lives during the First World War.
